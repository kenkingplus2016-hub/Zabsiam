Booking Terms

  • Prices shown are starting prices and may vary depending on guest numbers, menu selections and venue.
  • Premium ingredients may incur additional charges.
  • Parking, travel costs and London Congestion Charge are charged at cost.
  • Tableware, crockery, cutlery, tables and specialist equipment are not included unless agreed in advance.
  • Bookings must be made at least 7 days in advance.
  • A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining 50% balance must be paid at least 3 days (72 hours) prior to the event date.
  • Final guest numbers, menu selections and allergy information must be confirmed at least 5 days before the event.
  • Changes made after ingredients have been purchased may incur additional charges.
  • Please advise us of any allergies or dietary requirements before your event.
