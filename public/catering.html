Amuse-Bouche Collection

Mini Moo Ping on Khao Jee

Traditional Isaan Flavours, Reimagined: Tender charcoal-grilled marinated pork served on Khao Jee — traditional Northeastern Thai sticky rice, lightly coated with egg and grilled until golden and crisp on the outside while remaining soft within.

Finished with Zab Siam's signature Jaew Reduction, roasted rice, fresh herbs and delicate garnishes, this one-bite amuse-bouche offers a refined introduction to the bold and authentic flavours of Isaan cuisine.

Miang Kham Duo

A Taste of Thailand in Two Perfect Bites: A refined interpretation of Thailand's beloved Miang Kham, featuring two distinctive bites.

The first showcases crispy sea bass, while the second highlights succulent king prawn. Each is wrapped in fresh wild betel leaf with roasted coconut, peanuts, ginger, lime, chilli and Zab Siam's signature Miang Kham sauce, delivering the perfect balance of sweet, sour, salty and spicy flavours.

Kor Moo Yang Sushi Trilogy

Three handcrafted bites celebrating Thailand's iconic grilled pork neck.
Each bite is served on hand-pressed sticky rice and finished with a unique Thai flavour profile: Tom Yum, Larb, and Citrus. Garnished with regional herbs and fresh vegetables, then paired with Zab Siam's signature Jaew Reduction for a refined interpretation of Isaan cuisine.

Sai Ua & Nam Prik Noom

A Northern Thai Classic, Reimagined: Premium Northern Thai Sai Ua sausage, charcoal-grilled and served with Zab Siam's signature Nam Prik Noom. Accompanied by a selection of house-made Thai pickles, creating a balanced contrast of smoky, spicy, fresh and tangy flavours in every bite.

Thai Pickles Selection: Pickled Cucumber, Pickled Spring Onion, Pickled Solo Garlic, Pickled Ginger.

Finished with delicate micro herbs and certified edible flowers.

Crispy Rice Cracker & Salmon Pla

A Modern Thai Bite: Crispy jasmine rice cracker topped with finely diced fresh salmon, delicately dressed in Zab Siam's signature Pla dressing. Finished with cucumber, wild betel leaf, fresh lime, finely sliced kaffir lime leaves and red chilli, creating a vibrant balance of citrus, herbs and gentle heat in every bite.

Served as three handcrafted bites, finished with micro herbs and certified edible flowers.

Watermelon & Dried Fish

Modern Interpretation of a Thai Royal Classic: A modern interpretation of the classic Thai royal snack. A compressed watermelon sphere filled with Zab Siam's sweet dried fish mixture is presented on a crispy sweet and savoury crumble, finished with a delicate leaf tuile, chilli salt caviar pearls, micro herbs and edible flowers. A refined balance of sweetness, saltiness and refreshing freshness in a single elegant bite.
