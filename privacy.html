Zab Siam AI Team – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Zab Siam AI Team connects an authorised ChatGPT action to the Zab Siam Facebook Page. It processes only the Page content and interaction data needed to perform an action requested by an authorised administrator.

Data processed

The service may process Page profile information, posts, comments, Messenger conversation identifiers and messages, and Page insights. Authentication credentials are stored as protected server environment variables and are not returned to ChatGPT.

Purpose and sharing

Data is used only to read or manage the Zab Siam Page at the administrator's request. It is not sold. It is shared only with service providers required to operate the service, including Meta, OpenAI, and the hosting provider.

Retention and security

The API does not intentionally persist Facebook content. Hosting and platform providers may retain operational logs under their own policies. Access is protected by authentication and restricted Page permissions.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time through Meta Business Integrations or by removing the server credentials. Requests concerning access or deletion may be sent to info@zabsiam.co.uk.
